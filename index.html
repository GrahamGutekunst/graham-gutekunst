About Graham

A Versatile Young Performer

Graham Kirk Gutekunst is a compelling young musical theatre artist based in the Raleigh, North Carolina area, with nearly a decade of experience bringing characters to life on stage. From broad comedic roles to richly dramatic performances, Graham brings genuine range and emotional honesty to every production.

Graham has appeared with North Raleigh Christian Academy, Theatre in the Park, NC Theatre Conservatory, and other local organizations. His strong stage presence, reliable character work, and infectious enthusiasm for storytelling have made him a standout performer across many productions.
